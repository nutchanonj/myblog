Since there are very few videos for Thai language learners to practice, I would like to recommend some of the Thai YouTube channels for those who are interested in practicing Thai listening/speaking skills. 

1.

This channel is "โบโบ กวนจีน 波波真幸福", the owner of this channel (names โบโบ: 波波) is a Chinese teacher in Thailand, and she makes a lot of content about traveling around Taiwan. The good sides of her videos are that there are Thai and traditional Chinese subtitles, as well as English subtitles if you configure them in the video settings. Also, I think the language used in those videos is very common in everyday life. Also, I think she speaks Thai relatively slowly in her videos, so it could be easier for you if you are still a beginner.

[Link](https://www.youtube.com/@kuanjeen)

2.

The channel "BACKPAEGER แบกเป้เกอร์". ซันนี่, the owner of this channel, makes videos about traveling around the world. 
Almost all of his videos have both Thai and English subtitles. (This is my favorite YouTube channel, by the way.)

[Link](https://www.youtube.com/@backpaeger)

3.

The channel "Go Went Go". ​บาส, the owner of this channel, also makes videos about traveling around the world. 
Almost all of his videos have both Thai and English subtitles.

[Link](https://www.youtube.com/@GoWentGo)

4.

The channel of บอย พิษณุ, an actor in Thailand. 
Most of his videos have both Thai and English subtitles.

[Link](https://www.youtube.com/@Bradboyofficial/)

5.

The channel "Point of View". Her name is วิว, and she makes videos about history and science.
Most videos have Thai subtitles, but no English subtitles.

[Link](https://www.youtube.com/@PointofView)

6.

The channel "SpokeDark TV". The owner of this channel, named จอห์น วิญญู, mostly makes videos about Thai politics. He is inclined more to the liberal side of politics. 
Most videos have Thai subtitles, but no English subtitles.

[Link](https://www.youtube.com/@spokedarktv)

7.

The channel "Mahidol Channel มหิดล แชนแนล" This is the channel of Mahidol University of Thailand. Most videos relate to medicine and health. Very high-quality content here!
Most videos have Thai subtitles, but no English subtitles.

[Link](https://www.youtube.com/@mahidolchannel)

8.

This is the playlist of the "Club Friday Show", the interviews about the love lives of Thai famous people. On-screen Thai subtitle, but no English subtitle.

[Link](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLc3B9DM2kKP51_wI0qfCADapvhgjIH6eY)

9.

The channel of the famous Thai radio host น้าเน๊ก, who has turned into a YouTuber. On-screen Thai subtitle, but no English subtitle.

[Link](https://www.youtube.com/@NANAKE555)
