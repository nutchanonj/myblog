Since there are very few videos for Thai language learners to practice, I would like to recommend some of Thai YouTube channels for those who interest in practice Thai listening/speaking skills. 

1.

This channel is "โบโบ กวนจีน 波波真幸福", the owner of this channel (names โบโบ: 波波) is Chinese teacher in Thailand, and she makes a lot of contents about traveling around Taiwan. The good sides for her videos are that there are Thai and Traditional Chinese subtitles, and also English subtitle if you configure that in the video setting, and also, I think, the language used in those videos are very common in everyday life. Also, I think she speak Thai relatively slow in her videos, so it could be more easy for you if you are still the beginner.

[Link](https://www.youtube.com/@kuanjeen)

2.

The channel "BACKPAEGER แบกเป้เกอร์". ซันนี่ , the owner of this channel makes video about traveling around the world. 
Almost of his videos have both Thai and English subtitles. (This is my most favorite YouTube channel, by the way.)

[Link](https://www.youtube.com/@backpaeger)

3.

The channel "Go Went Go". ​บาส, the owner of this channel also makes video about traveling around the world. 
Almost of his videos have both Thai and English subtitles.

[Link](https://www.youtube.com/@GoWentGo)

4.

The channel of บอย พิษณุ; An actor in Thailand. 
Most of his videos have both Thai and English subtitles.

[Link](https://www.youtube.com/@Bradboyofficial/)

5.

The channel "Point of View". Her names is วิว, and she makes videos about history and science.
Most videos have Thai subtitle, but no English subtitle.

[Link](https://www.youtube.com/@PointofView)

6.

The channel "SpokeDark TV". The owner of this channel names จอห์น วิญญู mostly makes videos about Thai politics. He is inclined more to liberal side of politics. 
Most videos have Thai subtitle, but no English subtitle.

[Link](https://www.youtube.com/@spokedarktv)

7.

The channel "Mahidol Channel มหิดล แชนแนล" This is the channel of Mahidol University of Thailand. Most videos relate to medical and health. Very high quality contents here!
Most videos have Thai subtitle, but no English subtitle.

[Link](https://www.youtube.com/@mahidolchannel)

This is the playlist of the "Club Friday Show", The interviews about love life of Thai famous people. On-screen Thai subtitle but no English subtitle.
